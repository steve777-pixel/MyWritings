"The Silent Patient" novel review 

I have recently read "The Silent Patient" by Alex Michaelides and decided to write a reflection on this book. I do not usually write about what I read, but if I do, it means it is a great book. 

The book was indeed a brilliant psychological thriller and detective story. The story is about Theo and how he helps a famous, or rather infamous, artist woman Alicia Berenson deal with her emotional traumas after her husband's death. 

Alicia Berenson - the patient in the novel - is accused of murdering her husband by shooting him five times in the head in her house. But when questioned after the murder, she does not speak a word, turning completely mute. Nobody will be able to get a word out of her mouth, until Theo - the protagonist of the story - shows up. 

Theo is a psychotherapist who decides to take the responsibility of Alicia Berenson and make her speak. After finding out about her background, he believes Alicia actually didn't kill her husband and she was just a witness. Theo starts to investigate Alicia's story, reads her diary, and questions her neighbors and relatives. At the same time, he conducts therapy sessions with Alicia, though they always ended up Theo speaking to himself with no reaction from Alicia. 

As Theo uncovers more about Alicia, the story gets more tense and Alicia starts to speak and tells Theo how the murder actually happened. However, Theo hardly finds her words believable. 

When Alicia was about to speak the most important thing, someone tries to get rid of her. Their attempts turns out unsuccessful, although Alicia is left in a terminal condition. 

The book ends with an amazing plot twist which almost no reader can guess. It was so surprising, it took me some time for an emotional reset after reading the plot twist. I just said "What the f*ck?" 

The story was amazing. Opened the book in the morning, struggled to put it down, and finished in the evening.
